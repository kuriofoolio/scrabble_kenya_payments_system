Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my payment fail?

Payments can fail for various reasons, including:

  • Insufficient funds in your account
  • Incorrect card information
  • Transaction declined by your bank
  • Network or connectivity issues
  • Payment gateway timeout

Will I be charged for a failed payment?

No, you will not be charged for a failed payment. However, some banks may temporarily hold the amount as a pending transaction. This hold is usually released within 3-5 business days. If you notice any unexpected charges, please contact your bank.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept a variety of payment methods through our Pesapal payment gateway, including:

  • M-Pesa
  • Airtel Money
  • Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • Bank transfers (selected banks)
